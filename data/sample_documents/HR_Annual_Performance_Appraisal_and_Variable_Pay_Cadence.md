## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the annual performance appraisal cycle, 360-degree feedback framework, performance rating distribution guidelines, and variable pay (bonus) disbursement structure for all full-time employees of Cortex Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

This policy applies to all permanent employees (confirmed) across Engineering, HR, Finance, and all other departments. Contract employees, interns, and probationary employees (within first 6 months) are excluded from the variable pay structure but may participate in the development feedback cycle at manager discretion.

---

## 2. Performance Cycle Overview

### 2.1 Annual Appraisal Calendar

Cortex follows a **January–December fiscal year performance cycle** with two formal touchpoints:

| Event | Period | Month |
|-------|--------|-------|
| H1 Mid-Year Check-In (MYR) | January 1 – June 30 performance | **July (1st–15th)** |
| H2 Annual Review (Year-End) | Full year (Jan 1 – Dec 31) | **December (1st)–January (15th)** |
| Year-End Rating Finalization | Calibration and moderation complete | **January 31st** |
| Variable Pay (Bonus) Disbursement | With March payroll | **March 31st** |

### 2.2 Mid-Year Review (H1 — July)

The mid-year review is a **developmental and course-correction exercise**, not a rating exercise. Outcomes include:
- Progress review against H1 KRAs (Key Result Areas) and OKRs
- Development feedback on competencies and behavioral goals
- Manager-guided career conversation (aspiration and growth mapping)
- Identification of employees requiring support (early PIP flag, if warranted)

> **No formal rating or bell-curve distribution is applied at the mid-year review.** The mid-year conversation is recorded in Darwinbox (HRMS) as a manager-drafted narrative.

### 2.3 Year-End Annual Review (H2 — December/January)

The year-end review is the **formal, rated appraisal** that determines performance ratings and variable pay eligibility.

**Timeline:**
1. **December 1–10:** Employee Self-Appraisal submission in Darwinbox
2. **December 10–20:** 360-degree feedback collection (peers, skip-level, cross-functional)
3. **December 20–31:** Manager Assessment and rating submission
4. **January 1–15:** Calibration moderation sessions (Dept. Heads + HR)
5. **January 15–31:** Rating finalization, employee communication, and sign-off
6. **March 31:** Variable pay disbursement with payroll

## 3. Performance Rating Scale

Cortex uses a **5-point rating scale** for the year-end annual appraisal:

| Rating | Label | Description | Bell Curve Target |
|--------|-------|-------------|------------------|
| **5** | **Outstanding (OT)** | Consistently and significantly exceeds all KRAs and OKRs. Delivers transformational impact. Role model for the department. | ~5% of employees |
| **4** | **Exceeds Expectations (EE)** | Exceeds most KRAs with high-quality outcomes and meaningful cross-functional impact. | ~15% of employees |
| **3** | **Meets Expectations (ME)** | Fully delivers all assigned KRAs and OKRs with consistent, solid performance. Standard for the majority. | ~60% of employees |
| **2** | **Needs Improvement (NI)** | Partially meets KRAs. Areas of concern identified. Improvement plan initiated. | ~15% of employees |
| **1** | **Unacceptable (UA)** | Fails to meet core expectations despite prior feedback and support. Performance Improvement Plan mandatory. | ~5% of employees |

### 3.1 Bell Curve Distribution Guidelines

The HR team and department heads apply a **calibrated bell-curve distribution** during the January calibration moderation sessions to ensure normalization across teams of different sizes.

**Target Distribution:**
- **Outstanding (5) + Exceeds (4):** Combined not to exceed **20%** of the department headcount
- **Meets Expectations (3):** Target band of **60–70%** of department headcount
- **Needs Improvement (2) + Unacceptable (1):** Combined minimum **10–15%** of department headcount

> **Important:** Bell-curve distributions are targets/guidelines, not rigid quotas. In exceptional cases (e.g., newly formed high-performing teams, departments undergoing organizational changes), HR-approved deviations are permissible with documented justification from the Department Head.

## 4. 360-Degree Feedback Process

### 4.1 Feedback Sources

For the year-end review, each employee receives structured developmental feedback from:

| Feedback Source | Who Provides | Mode |
|----------------|-------------|------|
| Manager Assessment | Direct reporting manager | Mandatory, rated + narrative |
| Peer Feedback | 3–5 same-level peers (employee nominates, manager approves) | Anonymous, narrative only |
| Skip-Level Review | Manager's manager | Mandatory for Rating 4/5 and Rating 1/2 |
| Cross-Functional Feedback | 1–2 stakeholders from other departments | Optional, encouraged for collaborative roles |
| Self-Appraisal | Employee self-assessment | Mandatory |

### 4.2 Feedback Principles

- Peer feedback is **anonymized** — the feedback giver's identity is not disclosed to the appraisee
- Feedback must be **specific, behavioral, and outcome-linked** (no generic statements)
- HR will reject feedback submissions that contain personal, discriminatory, or non-work-related content
- Feedback collected is used for **development purposes only** and informs (not determines) the manager's rating

## 5. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

### 5.1 PIP Eligibility

A PIP is initiated for employees who receive a year-end rating of **Unacceptable (1)** or who, following a mid-year flag, have not demonstrated adequate improvement.

A PIP may also be initiated during the performance cycle (outside the appraisal cycle) for sustained poor performance as determined by the manager and approved by HR.

### 5.2 PIP Structure

| Phase | Duration | Activity |
|-------|---------|---------|
| PIP Initiation | Day 1 | PIP document drafted jointly by Manager + HR, reviewed with employee |
| Active PIP Period | **60 calendar days** | Bi-weekly check-in meetings (mandatory), measurable targets tracked |
| Final Assessment | Day 60 | Manager + HR review outcomes against PIP targets |

**PIP Outcomes:**
- **Successful completion:** PIP closed, employee continues with enhanced support. Rating reviewed at next cycle.
- **Partial improvement:** PIP extended by 30 days (maximum one extension, subject to HR approval)
- **Unsuccessful PIP:** Escalated to HR and Department Head for further action, which may include termination as per applicable law and employment contract terms

> All PIP documents, check-in notes, and outcomes must be formally documented in Darwinbox. Verbal PIP agreements without documentation are invalid.

### 5.3 Employee Rights During PIP

- The employee has the right to request an **HR representative** to be present at any PIP meeting
- The employee may submit written objections to any PIP target within 5 business days of PIP initiation
- The employee may escalate to the HR Head if they believe the PIP is initiated in bad faith

## 6. Variable Pay (Performance Bonus) Structure

### 6.1 Eligibility

Variable pay is applicable to all permanent full-time employees who:
- Have completed **at least 6 months of service** (confirmed) by December 31st of the appraisal year
- Are **actively employed** on the date of disbursement (March 31st) — employees who resign or are terminated before disbursement are not eligible, unless their employment agreement specifies otherwise
- Have not received an Unacceptable (1) or Needs Improvement (2) rating

### 6.2 Variable Pay Calculation

Variable pay is calculated as:

**Variable Pay Disbursed = Target Variable Pay × Performance Multiplier**

Where:
- **Target Variable Pay** = Percentage of CTC as defined in the employee's offer letter (typically 10%–25% of annual CTC depending on grade/band)
- **Performance Multiplier** = Based on year-end rating (see table below)

### 6.3 Performance Multiplier Table

| Year-End Rating | Label | Multiplier |
|----------------|-------|-----------|
| 5 — Outstanding | OT | **1.5x** |
| 4 — Exceeds Expectations | EE | **1.2x** |
| 3 — Meets Expectations | ME | **1.0x** |
| 2 — Needs Improvement | NI | **0.0x** (ineligible) |
| 1 — Unacceptable | UA | **0.0x** (ineligible) |

**Example Calculation:**
- Employee CTC: ₹24,00,000 per annum
- Target Variable: 15% of CTC = ₹3,60,000
- Year-End Rating: 4 (Exceeds Expectations) → Multiplier 1.2x
- **Variable Pay Disbursed: ₹3,60,000 × 1.2 = ₹4,32,000** (disbursed with March payroll)

### 6.4 Proration for Mid-Year Joiners

Employees who joined between July 1 and December 31 (but completed 6 months by March 31 of next year) receive variable pay prorated based on months of service:

**Prorated Variable Pay = Full Variable Pay × (Months Completed / 12)**

## 7. Grievance and Appeals

Employees who disagree with their performance rating may initiate a formal appeal:
1. Submit written appeal to HR within **10 business days** of receiving the final rating communication
2. HR coordinates a **Review Panel** (HR Head + Department Head + Skip-Level Manager)
3. Review Panel provides a decision within **15 business days** of appeal submission
4. Review Panel's decision is **final and binding**

Employees raising a grievance will not face any form of retaliation. Any manager found to have retaliated against an employee for filing an appeal will be subject to disciplinary action.
