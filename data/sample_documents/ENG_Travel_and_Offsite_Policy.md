# Engineering Department Travel and Technical Offsite Policy (2026)

**Document ID:** POL-ENG-2026-003  
**Department:** Engineering  
**Security Clearance Level:** Internal  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Reviewed:** January 15, 2026  
**Target Audience:** Software Engineers, Site Reliability Engineers (SREs), Engineering Managers, Tech Leads  
**Policy Owner:** VP of Engineering  

---

## 1. Scope & Objective

This policy outlines the travel, accommodation, and per diem standards for all engineering personnel traveling for technical hackathons, architecture offsites, customer on-site architectural reviews, or developer conferences (e.g., PyCon India, Rootconf, GopherCon India, AWS Community Day).

Engineering recognizes that intense technical summits, cross-facility syncs between our primary engineering hubs—**Bengaluru (Bellandur Tech Corridor)**, **Hyderabad (HITEC City)**, and **Pune (Hinjawadi Phase 1)**—require comfortable and productive conditions for our engineering talent.

---

## 2. Daily Meal & Incidental Allowance (Per Diem)

Engineering personnel traveling on approved business or attending engineering offsites are entitled to an enhanced daily meal and incidental allowance:

- **Daily Meal Allowance:** **₹2,500 per day** (non-taxable per diem allowance covering breakfast, lunch, dinner, and non-alcoholic beverages).
- **Incidentals & Refreshments:** Light working snacks and specialty coffee during technical sprints are covered under the ₹2,500/day pool.
- **Offsite Team Dinners:** Pre-arranged group technical dinners hosted by an Engineering Manager or Director with 4 or more engineers may be expensed separately up to ₹3,500 per head with itemized GST invoices.
- **No Individual Itemization Needed for Per Diem:** Individual meal receipts are not required when claiming within the standard ₹2,500/day per diem ceiling, provided travel dates match approved engineering travel orders.

---

## 3. Hotel Accommodations & Lodging Limits

Accommodation limits are calibrated to ensure engineers reside within a 20-minute transit perimeter of primary tech hubs to avoid Bengaluru/Mumbai traffic bottlenecks:

- **Tier-1 Metropolitan Cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Gurugram, Delhi NCR):** Up to **₹6,500 per night** (excluding statutory GST).
  - Recommended business hotels: Partnered Marriott Courtyard (Outer Ring Road, Bengaluru), Lemon Tree Premier (HITEC City / Gurugram), or Radisson Blu.
- **Tier-2 Tech Clusters (Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad):** Up to **₹5,000 per night** (excluding GST).
- **Extended Technical Residencies:** For sprint assignments exceeding 14 continuous days, engineering teams must coordinate with Workplace Services for serviced business apartment leasing (e.g., Oakwood or equivalent).

---

## 4. Ground Transportation & Commute

To maximize engineering productivity and ensure physical safety during late-night production support or deployment offsites:

- **Uber / Ola for Business:** All active engineers are provisioned with an **Uber for Business / Ola Corporate** linked profile using their corporate email. Airport pickups, inter-hub travel between tech centers, and client site visits must be charged directly to the `Engineering-Cost-Center` profile.
- **Airport Transfers:** Airport transit between Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM), or Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) and corporate centers is fully covered via corporate cab profiles or pre-paid airport taxi counters (up to ₹2,200 each way).
- **Local Micro-Transit:** Auto-rickshaw (Namma Yatri / meter auto) or metro journeys for intra-city commute are claimable up to ₹800 per day against UPI transaction screenshots.

---

## 5. Air & Train Travel Classes

- **Domestic Air Travel:** Economy class on commercial carriers (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, Vistara). Flights exceeding 4 hours (e.g., Bengaluru to Srinagar/Guwahati) are eligible for extra legroom seat selection (₹800 to ₹1,200 fee covered).
- **Rail Travel:** For regional transit (e.g., Bengaluru to Chennai, Mumbai to Pune, Hyderabad to Vijayawada), **Vande Bharat Express (Executive Chair Car)** or **Tejas Express / Shatabdi 1st AC** is fully authorized.

---

## 6. Approvals & Reimbursement Workflow

1. All travel requests must be approved in advance by the respective Engineering Manager / Director via the internal ERP travel module.
2. For claims exceeding the standard allowance (e.g., last-minute surge pricing or high-occupancy conference weeks), an exception approval from the VP of Engineering is required.
3. Travel claims must be submitted within 21 calendar days following completion of travel.
