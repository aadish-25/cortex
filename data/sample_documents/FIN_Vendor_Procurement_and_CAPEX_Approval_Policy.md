## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the framework for vendor procurement, capital expenditure (CapEx) approval, and competitive bidding requirements for all departments of Cortex Technologies Pvt. Ltd. All employees, managers, department heads, and contractors responsible for initiating or approving purchases must adhere to this policy.

This policy applies to:
- Hardware and software procurement
- Cloud infrastructure and SaaS subscriptions
- Professional services and consulting engagements
- Office supplies, equipment, and facilities
- Any expenditure funded from approved departmental budgets

---

## 2. CapEx vs. OpEx Classification

### 2.1 Capital Expenditure (CapEx)

CapEx refers to funds used to acquire, upgrade, or maintain long-term physical or intangible assets. Under Companies Act 2013 and Indian GAAP, CapEx is capitalized and depreciated over the asset's useful life.

**CapEx Examples:**
- Server hardware, laptops, and workstations (useful life ≥ 3 years)
- Licensed software with perpetual rights (ERP, security tools)
- Office infrastructure improvements and leasehold improvements
- Data center networking equipment

**Depreciation Schedule (Indian GAAP — Companies Act 2013, Schedule II):**
| Asset Category | Useful Life | Depreciation Method |
|---------------|------------|-------------------|
| Computer Hardware | 3 years | Written Down Value (WDV) |
| Office Furniture | 10 years | Straight Line Method (SLM) |
| Leasehold Improvements | Lease term or 10 years | SLM |
| Licensed Software (Perpetual) | 3–5 years | SLM |

### 2.2 Operational Expenditure (OpEx)

OpEx refers to ongoing costs for running day-to-day operations, fully expensed in the period incurred.

**OpEx Examples:**
- SaaS/cloud subscriptions (AWS, Google Workspace, GitHub)
- Consulting and professional services engagements < 12 months
- Travel and entertainment
- Office supplies and consumables

## 3. Competitive Bidding Requirements

### 3.1 Mandatory 3-Quote Rule

For any **single purchase or contract exceeding ₹2,00,000 (Indian Rupees Two Lakhs)**, the requisitioning department must obtain a minimum of **three competitive quotations** from qualified vendors before initiating a Purchase Order (PO).

**Quotation Requirements:**
- Quotes must be from independent, unaffiliated vendors
- Quotes must be on vendor letterhead (PDF acceptable for established vendors)
- Quotes must be valid for a minimum of 30 days from the issue date
- Quotes must include itemized pricing, delivery timelines, warranty terms, and GST applicable

**Exemptions from 3-Quote Rule:**
1. **Sole-source procurement** — where only one vendor globally offers the required product/service. Requires documented justification and CFO approval.
2. **Emergency procurement** — for unplanned urgent business continuity needs. Requires post-facto 3-quote validation within 15 days.
3. **Existing empaneled vendors** — renewed contracts within previously approved rates do not require re-quoting if renewal is within 10% of prior contract value.

### 3.2 Request for Proposal (RFP) Process

For procurements exceeding ₹10,00,000 (Ten Lakhs), the Finance team will coordinate a formal **RFP process**:

| Phase | Activity | Timeline |
|-------|---------|---------|
| RFP Preparation | Finance + Dept. Head define scope, SLAs, evaluation criteria | Days 1–5 |
| RFP Issuance | Sent to minimum 5 empaneled or invited vendors | Day 6 |
| Vendor Q&A Period | Vendors submit clarifications | Days 7–14 |
| Proposal Submission Deadline | Sealed bids submitted | Day 21 |
| Technical Evaluation | Cross-functional evaluation committee scores proposals | Days 22–28 |
| Commercial Negotiation | Finance negotiates with top 2 shortlisted vendors | Days 29–35 |
| Award Decision | CFO signs final award letter | Day 36–40 |

## 4. Procurement Approval Matrix (Delegation of Authority)

All procurement requests must follow the Delegation of Authority (DoA) matrix. Approvals must be obtained **before** the Purchase Order is raised or any work commences.

### 4.1 CapEx Approval Matrix

| Expenditure Range | Approver(s) Required |
|------------------|---------------------|
| Up to ₹50,000 | Department Manager |
| ₹50,001 – ₹5,00,000 | Department Manager + Finance Controller |
| ₹5,00,001 – ₹25,00,000 | VP/Director (Business Unit) + Finance Controller |
| ₹25,00,001 and above | CFO (mandatory) + CEO (above ₹1,00,00,000) |

### 4.2 OpEx / Recurring Subscription Approval Matrix

| Expenditure Range (Annual Value) | Approver(s) Required |
|----------------------------------|---------------------|
| Up to ₹1,00,000 | Department Manager |
| ₹1,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | VP/Director |
| ₹10,00,001 – ₹50,00,000 | CFO |
| Above ₹50,00,000 | CFO + CEO |

> **Note:** Annual contract values are computed on the total commitment over the contract term. Multi-year contracts are evaluated at their total contract value for DoA purposes.

## 5. Vendor Onboarding and Due Diligence

### 5.1 Vendor Empanelment Requirements

All new vendors must complete the **Vendor Onboarding Form** and submit the following mandatory documents before any PO can be raised:

| Document | Requirement |
|---------|------------|
| GST Registration Certificate | Active GSTIN mandatory |
| PAN Card (Company/Individual) | Mandatory |
| Bank Account Verification Letter (Cancelled Cheque) | Mandatory |
| MSME Registration (if applicable) | Recommended for preferential payment terms |
| ISO/SOC 2 Certification (for technology vendors) | Mandatory for vendors handling company data |
| Signed NDA/Confidentiality Agreement | Mandatory before any proposal phase |

### 5.2 Vendor Due Diligence

Finance conducts the following due diligence checks:
- **MCA21 / RoC search** — Verify company registration status and director details
- **CIBIL/Equifax credit check** — For vendors with deferred payment terms
- **Past client references** — Minimum 2 reference checks for contracts above ₹5,00,000
- **Blacklist screening** — Cross-check against government debarred vendor lists

## 6. Purchase Order and Invoicing Process

### 6.1 Purchase Order Creation

POs must be raised in the ERP system (Zoho Books) with the following mandatory fields:
- Vendor GSTIN and PAN
- Itemized line items with HSN/SAC codes
- Delivery timeline commitment
- Payment terms (see Section 6.3)
- Budget code and cost center

### 6.2 GST Invoice Requirements

**All vendor invoices must comply with GST rules under CGST Act 2017:**
- Invoice must carry the company's registered GSTIN: `29AABCC1234F1Z5`
- HSN/SAC code must be present for every line item
- E-invoice (IRN + QR code) mandatory for vendors with annual turnover >₹5 Crore
- Input Tax Credit (ITC) will only be claimed for invoices where the vendor has filed GSTR-1 and credit appears in GSTR-2B

### 6.3 Payment Terms

| Vendor Category | Standard Payment Terms |
|----------------|----------------------|
| Technology SaaS / Subscriptions | Net 30 (prepayment for annual subscriptions) |
| Consulting / Professional Services | Net 45 from invoice date |
| Hardware / Equipment Vendors | Net 30 from delivery + acceptance sign-off |
| MSME-registered vendors | Net 45 (mandatory per MSMED Act 2006, any delay attracts 3x RBI rate interest) |

**All payments require:**
1. Original GST-compliant tax invoice received by Finance
2. Delivery receipt / services acceptance confirmation signed by Department Head
3. 3-way match completed in ERP (PO → GRN → Invoice)
4. Approval email from authorized signatory per DoA matrix

## 7. Policy Violations and Consequences

Procurement initiated without following this policy constitutes a **Policy Violation** and may result in:

- Personal liability for the employee for any committed expense
- Disciplinary action per HR Code of Conduct Policy
- Non-reimbursement of personal expenditures incurred outside the approval process
- Audit escalation to the Audit Committee for amounts exceeding ₹5,00,000
