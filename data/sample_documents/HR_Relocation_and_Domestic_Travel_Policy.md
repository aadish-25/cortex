# Human Resources Policy: Talent Relocation, Transfer Support, and Domestic Travel Guidelines

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2026-007  
**Department:** Human Resources (HR)  
**Security Clearance Level:** Internal  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Reviewed:** January 15, 2026  
**Target Audience:** All Permanent Employees, Relocating Hires, People Partners, Talent Acquisition Specialists  
**Policy Owner:** Head of HR & Talent Operations  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

Cortex Technologies India is committed to attracting, retaining, and supporting exceptional talent across the subcontinent. Whether expanding our engineering core in **Bengaluru**, establishing financial operations in **Mumbai** or **Pune**, or staffing our corporate hub in **Gurugram** and **Hyderabad**, this policy provides equitable relocation assistance, transition lodging, and domestic travel allowances.

---

## 2. Daily Meal & Incidental Allowance for HR Assignments & Relocation

Employees traveling on HR-sponsored assignments (e.g., campus hiring at IITs/NITs/IIMs, offsite HR mediation, or transitioning to a new hub):

- **Daily Meal Allowance:** **₹1,800 per day** (flat per diem pool for domestic travel and temporary relocation transition days).
- **Meal Coverage:** Recommended daily distribution of ₹400 for breakfast, ₹600 for lunch, and ₹800 for dinner.
- **Transitional Relocation Per Diem:** The ₹1,800/day meal allowance is claimable for the relocating employee for up to the first **fourteen (14) calendar days** while residing in company-provided guest accommodations.
- Direct family members accompanying the employee during relocation do not receive separate meal per diems; family transition expenses are encompassed within the relocation lump-sum grant.

---

## 3. Relocation Lump-Sum Assistance

To assist candidates and transferring employees with inter-city moving expenses, broker deposits, and household setup:

### 3.1 Relocation Grant
- **Inter-City Relocation Lump Sum:** **₹75,000 (Seventy-Five Thousand Indian Rupees)** paid directly to the employee in the first monthly payroll cycle following their official joining or transfer date.
- **Applicable Hubs:** Applies to all permanent employees transferring from outside the base city region to **Bengaluru (Bellandur/Whitefield/Koramangala)**, **Gurugram (Cyber City)**, **Mumbai**, **Hyderabad**, or **Pune**.
- **Intended Utilization:** The ₹75,000 grant covers rental agreement registration, brokerage fees, local transportation, utility hookups, and miscellaneous settling-in expenses.
- **Taxation Note:** Relocation allowances are disbursed subject to applicable Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) provisions under the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961, unless supported by valid statutory expenditure proofs submitted to Payroll.

### 3.2 Household Goods Transit & Packing
In addition to the ₹75,000 lump sum, the Company provides direct empanelment and settlement with approved packers and movers:
- **Empaneled Logistics Partners:** Agarwal Packers & Movers (DRS Group) or Leo Packers.
- **Coverage Ceiling:** Direct company billing up to **₹50,000** for door-to-door household packing, road transit, loading/unloading, and comprehensive transit insurance.

---

## 4. Temporary Guest House Accommodations

To provide a stress-free transition while searching for permanent residential leasing:

- **Company Guest House Stay:** The Company provides up to **14 consecutive calendar days** of complimentary accommodation in Company-managed executive guest houses or partnered serviced apartments located in Bengaluru (Indiranagar / Koramangala / Bellandur) or Gurugram (DLF Phase 2 / Golf Course Road).
- **Extension Requests:** If extreme rental availability constraints occur, an employee may request an extension of up to seven (7) additional days with written approval from the Head of HR Operations. Guest house stays beyond 14 days without approved justification will be charged at a subsidized rate of ₹2,500/night deducted from salary.
- **Facilities Included:** Wi-Fi, daily housekeeping, breakfast, and 24/7 security.

---

## 5. Travel & Flight Entitlements for Relocation

- **One-Way Flight Tickets:** Economy class commercial flights (IndiGo, Air India, Vistara) for the employee, their legally married spouse, and dependent children under 18 years from their originating domestic airport to the destination hub.
- Excess baggage allowance up to 15 kg per traveling family member is covered upon submission of the airline baggage receipt.

---

## 6. Retention Covenant & Recovery Terms

1. **12-Month Service Agreement:** Employees receiving relocation assistance (the ₹75,000 lump sum, guest house lodging, and packing/moving billing) agree to remain in continuous active service with the Company for a minimum of **twelve (12) calendar months** from the effective relocation date.
2. **Pro-Rata Clawback:** In the event of voluntary resignation or termination for cause within 12 months, the full cost of relocation assistance will be recovered pro-rata from the employee's Full & Final (F&F) settlement and Gratuity/PF dues, in accordance with applicable Indian labor regulations.
