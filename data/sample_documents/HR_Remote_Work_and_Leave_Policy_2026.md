## 1. Executive Summary & Statutory Alignment

Cortex Technologies India Private Limited ("the Company") is dedicated to fostering a supportive, productive, and progressive workplace. This policy governs employee leave entitlements, remote and hybrid work support, home office ergonomics, and statutory social security benefits.

This document adheres strictly to statutory provisions under Indian employment legislation, including the **Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017**, the **Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952**, the **Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972**, and relevant State **Shops and Commercial Establishments Acts** across Karnataka (Bengaluru), Maharashtra (Mumbai/Pune), Haryana (Gurugram), and Telangana (Hyderabad).

## 2. Leave Entitlements & Accrual Rules

### 2.1 Annual Leave (Privilege Leave / Earned Leave)
- **Baseline Entitlement:** All regular full-time employees are entitled to **24 working days of annual leave** per calendar year, accrued at the rate of 2.0 working days per completed month of active service.
- **Carryover & Lapsing:** Up to a maximum of **8 unused annual leave days** may be carried forward into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be utilized on or before March 31 of the new calendar year. Any carried-over balance remaining after March 31 will automatically lapse without financial compensation.
- **Leave Encashment:** Unutilized accumulated annual leave (up to a ceiling of 30 days) is eligible for encashment upon separation as part of the Full & Final (F&F) settlement, computed on basic salary pursuant to applicable State Shops and Commercial Establishments guidelines.

### 2.2 Casual & Sick Leave (CSL)
- **Entitlement:** All employees receive **12 working days of casual/sick leave** per calendar year (credited in full on January 1 or pro-rata upon joining).
- **Casual Leave Guidelines:** Intended for unforeseen personal obligations. May be availed up to two (2) consecutive days with prior managerial notice.
- **Sick Leave Guidelines:** Intended for recovery from acute illnesses. Absences extending beyond two (2) consecutive calendar days require a valid medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner (MBBS or equivalent registered with the National Medical Commission).
- **Non-Accumulative:** Casual and sick leave cannot be carried over into subsequent years and is non-encashable upon separation.

## 3. Parental, Maternity, and Caregiver Benefits

### 3.1 Maternity Benefit (Maternity Benefit Amendment Act, 2017)
- **Duration:** Female employees with at least 80 days of active service in the 12 months preceding their expected date of delivery are entitled to **26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave** (for up to two surviving children). For female employees having two or more surviving children, the entitlement is 12 weeks.
- **Commencement:** Maternity leave may commence up to eight (8) weeks prior to the expected delivery date.
- **Additional Statutory Provisions:**
  - Additional one month paid leave in cases of illness arising out of pregnancy or delivery.
  - Creche facility access or a monthly creche allowance of ₹6,000 for mothers with children under 6 years of age.
  - Two nursing breaks daily until the child attains the age of 15 months.

### 3.2 Paternity Leave
- Male employees and secondary caregivers are entitled to **10 working days of fully paid paternity leave**, usable within six (6) months of childbirth or legal adoption.

### 3.3 Adoption & Surrogacy Leave
- Biological mothers commissioning surrogacy or adoptive mothers legally adopting an infant under the age of three months are entitled to **12 weeks of fully paid leave**.

## 4. Remote Work Framework & Home Office Stipends

Cortex operates under a flexible, distributed-first and hybrid collaboration posture across our Indian engineering and operations hubs.

### 4.1 Core Working Hours
To facilitate synchronous sprint coordination, code reviews, cross-team standups, and customer incident support, employees must maintain availability during:
- **Core Hours:** **10:00 AM – 4:00 PM IST (Indian Standard Time)**, Monday through Friday.
- Flexible schedule balancing is permitted between 08:00 AM and 08:00 PM IST with managerial concurrence.

### 4.2 One-Time Home Office Setup Reimbursement
To ensure an ergonomic, productive, and safe home working environment:
- **Reimbursement Grant:** All permanent remote and hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time reimbursement of up to **₹35,000 (Thirty-Five Thousand Indian Rupees)**.
- **Approved Purchases:**
  - Ergonomic office chair (mesh back, adjustable lumbar support).
  - Height-adjustable study desk or ergonomic standing desk converter.
  - External computer monitor (24-inch or 27-inch 2K/4K display).
  - Ergonomic keyboard, vertical mouse, monitor riser arm, and laptop cooling stand.
  - Surge-protected power strip and UPS battery backup for router/laptop.
- **Claim Process:** Must be submitted through the internal expense portal with a valid GST invoice within 90 days of employment commencement.

### 4.3 Monthly Broadband Internet Reimbursement
- **Monthly Allowance:** Up to **₹1,500 per month** to cover high-speed fiber broadband (e.g., Airtel Xstream Fiber, JioFiber, ACT Fibernet).
- **Submission Requirement:** Monthly tax invoice from the telecom service provider showing employee address and connection details.

## 5. Statutory Retirement & Social Security Provisions

### 5.1 Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)
- The Company and employee each make mandatory monthly contributions equal to **12% of the statutory wage ceiling / basic salary** to the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) pursuant to the EPF & MP Act, 1952. Employees may opt for Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF) contributions.

### 5.2 Payment of Gratuity
- Employees completing **five (5) continuous years of service** with the Company become eligible for statutory gratuity payments under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, calculated as `(15 * Last Drawn Basic Salary * Years of Service) / 26`.
