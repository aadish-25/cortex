# Global Corporate Finance Policy: Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement

**Policy Identifier:** POL-FIN-2026-005  
**Department:** Finance & Accounting  
**Clearance Level:** Internal  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Reviewed:** January 15, 2026  
**Audience:** All Company Employees, Contractors, Traveling Staff, Line Managers  
**Policy Owner:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO) & Head of Financial Operations  

---

## 1. Purpose and Financial Governance

Cortex Technologies India Private Limited ("the Company") is committed to rigorous financial discipline, statutory tax compliance, and maximizing Input Tax Credit (ITC) under the Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) regime.

This policy establishes mandatory expenditure ceilings, corporate booking channels, and strict statutory invoicing requirements for all employee business travel and corporate reimbursements across our hubs in **Bengaluru**, **Mumbai**, **Gurugram**, **Hyderabad**, and **Pune**.

---

## 2. Mandatory Corporate Booking Platform: MakeMyTrip Corporate Portal

### 2.1 Centralized Booking Mandate
All corporate travel arrangements—including domestic air tickets, railway bookings, and hotel reservations—must be booked exclusively through the official **MakeMyTrip Corporate (MMT MyBiz)** enterprise portal.

- Bookings made through personal consumer apps (MakeMyTrip consumer, Goibibo, Cleartrip, EaseMyTrip) or direct counter bookings are **strictly non-reimbursable**, except under verified emergency circumstances with prior written sign-off from the Head of Financial Operations.
- The MakeMyTrip Corporate portal is pre-configured with the Company's corporate GSTINs, automatic corporate discounting, and centralized direct billing to the corporate treasury account.

---

## 3. Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem Caps)

To maintain uniformity and control operating expenditures across all departments:

- **Daily Meal Allowance Cap:** **₹1,500 per day** maximum for all domestic business travel across India.
- **Breakdown Guidelines:** Breakfast: ₹300, Lunch: ₹500, Dinner: ₹700 (or cumulative ₹1,500 across 24 hours).
- **Prohibited Claims:** Personal minibar items, alcoholic beverages, tobacco products, and room entertainment charges are strictly personal expenditures and will be automatically deducted from reimbursement vouchers.
- **Client Entertainment:** Business meals with prospective clients or vendors exceeding ₹1,500 per person require prior expense pre-authorization from the Department Head and Finance Controller, supported by a formal attendee roster and business justification.

---

## 4. Hotel Accommodation Ceilings

Corporate hotel limits are enforced across all regional centers:

- **Standard Corporate Hotel Ceiling:** Up to **₹4,500 per night** (excluding statutory GST) across all cities, including metropolitan centers (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Pune).
- Bookings must be selected from the Company-negotiated corporate catalog within the MakeMyTrip Corporate portal.
- Any booking exceeding ₹4,500 per night requires prior written justification and written approval from the Financial Controller. Excess tariffs without pre-approval will be deducted from the employee's payroll settlement.

---

## 5. Strict Statutory Rule: Mandatory GST Invoice Requirement

### 5.1 The ₹500 GST Threshold Rule
In compliance with the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 and State GST provisions:

- **Mandatory GST Invoice:** Any reimbursement claim exceeding **₹500 (Five Hundred Indian Rupees)** must be accompanied by a valid, machine-generated **Tax Invoice** explicitly bearing the Company's full legal entity name and state-specific 15-digit GST Identification Number (GSTIN):
  - **Karnataka (Bengaluru):** `29AAACC1234F1Z5`
  - **Maharashtra (Mumbai/Pune):** `27AAACC1234F1Z9`
  - **Haryana (Gurugram):** `06AAACC1234F1Z1`
  - **Telangana (Hyderabad):** `36AAACC1234F1Z3`
- Handwritten kachha slips, photocopies, thermal slips with missing seller GSTINs, or generic POS credit card charge slips are **strictly inadmissible** for claims above ₹500.
- For claims under ₹500 (e.g., local auto-rickshaw fare or incidentals where no GST invoice can be generated), a digital UPI transaction receipt along with origin-destination log is required.

### 5.2 Input Tax Credit (ITC) Protection
Vendors must accurately file their GSTR-1 returns so that tax payments appear in the Company's GSTR-2B statement. Expense claims from vendors whose GST registration is flagged as cancelled, inactive, or non-compliant will be rejected.

---

## 6. Expense Submission Timelines & Auditing

1. **Submission Window:** All expense reimbursement reports must be submitted via the ERP system within **fifteen (15) calendar days** of travel completion. Reports submitted after 30 days will be permanently forfeited.
2. **Internal Audit Verification:** Finance conducts random audits on 100% of expense claims above ₹10,000 and a 20% randomized sample across all expense reports. Any submission of fabricated bills or tampered invoices constitutes criminal fraud and will result in immediate termination and legal recovery under the Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.
